Bitar CRM — The Simple Guide

Ye guide plain language me batati hai ki CRM me kya-kya hai, har cheez kya kaam karti hai aur kahaan milti hai — taaki aap khud samjho aur client ko confidently dikha sako. Technical terms nahi, sirf "kya hota hai".

🟢 Old = purane (v0) system me bhi tha  ·  🔵 New = ab naya add hua. Har module me "Kahaan" me menu ka naam diya hai.

Part A — Ek line me: CRM kya karta hai

Client ko yeh ek sentence bolna

"Ek customer ki mortgage enquiry aane se lekar loan disburse hone tak — har lead, document, bank application, approval aur paisa — ek jagah track hota hai, har role ko sirf apna kaam dikhta hai, aur har step ka record (audit) rehta hai."

Part B — Ek lead ki poori journey (kaun kya karta hai)

Yehi flow client ko demo karna — start se end tak
1
Advisor
Lead banata hai

Create Lead wizard bharta hai — customer ki detail + consent (call/WhatsApp allowed hai ya nahi). System duplicate check karta hai.

2
System (automatic)
Lead ko advisor ko de deta hai + score deta hai

Rules ke hisaab se advisor assign, ek score (0–100), aur turant bata deta hai loan eligible hai ya nahi (green/red). Ek 15-min "pehli call" ka timer chalu.

3
Advisor
Customer ko call/follow-up karta hai

"My Day" screen pe sabse zaroori kaam upar. Har baat ka follow-up log + agli call ki date. Abhi ready nahi toh "Nurture" pe daal do — baad me apne aap yaad dila dega.

4
Advisor → Ops
Documents collect + verify

Advisor documents upload karta hai. Ops verify/reject (reason ke saath) karta hai. Reject hua toh advisor ko dobara maangne ka task automatic.

5
Compliance Officer
KYC + screening

Compliance sanctions/PEP check karta hai, risk rating deta hai, aur KYC pass karta hai. Jab tak ye na ho, file bank tak nahi jaa sakti. (Advisor khud KYC pass nahi kar sakta.)

6
Advisor → Ops Manager
File Operations ko handover

Sab complete ho toh advisor "Submit to Operations" dabata hai. Adhura ho toh system rok deta hai. Ops Manager ek Ops Executive ko file deta hai.

7
Ops Executive
Bank(s) me submit + track

Ek ya kai bank me parallel apply. Bank ki query, follow-up, pre-approval, valuation, FOL terms — sab record. Chup-chaap 3–7 din ho gaye toh alert.

8
Ops → Accounts / CEO
Disbursement + revenue

Loan disburse → case complete, customer "Active Client" ban gaya, revenue CEO ke pipeline sheet me. Fixed-rate 120 din pehle khatam ho raha ho toh naya buyout lead apne aap.

Part C — Module by module (kya = kaam, kahaan = menu)

Client ko screen dikhate waqt yeh reference

Leads & Pipeline Old+

Leads list, pipeline kanban, sources, lost leads. Naya: eligibility check, lead score, consent, duplicate block, CSV import (preview + undo), saved filter views.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Leads
Pehle: sirf flat list. Ab: score/SLA/eligibility/consent sab lead ke andar.

My Day New

Advisor/Ops ki daily to-do — SLA-risk leads upar, phir overdue tasks, phir follow-ups. "Aaj kya karna hai" ek jagah.

Kahaan: Sidebar → My Day

Client 360 New

Ek customer ke saare cases, documents, bank applications, follow-ups aur consent history ek page pe. Customer permanent record ban gaya (pehle sirf leads the).

Kahaan: koi lead kholo → "Client 360" button

Documents Old+

Upload, verify/reject (reason), versioning, expiry alerts. Reject → advisor ko auto task.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Documents

Compliance & KYC New

KYC gate (sirf Compliance pass kare), sanctions/PEP screening, risk rating + EDD, confidential suspicion flags, data-subject requests. Ek dedicated Compliance workspace.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Compliance (Compliance/CEO login)

Operations New

Handover gate (adhura file rok deta hai), Ops queues (New Handovers, In Verification, Awaiting Bank, Query, Valuation, FOL, Transfer, On-Hold), dual ownership, silence escalation, structured FOL/valuation/pre-approval.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Ops Queue · lead ke andar "Application" tab

Bank Applications New

Ek case ko kai banks me parallel bhejo, har ek ka status/rate/query alag track.

Kahaan: lead → Application tab → Bank Applications

Approvals New

Jahaan approval chahiye (jaise advisor ne partner banaya) — request approver ke paas jaata hai. Jo request kare woh khud approve nahi kar sakta.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Approvals

Reports & Dashboards Old+

Har role ka apna dashboard. Report catalogue (9 reports) — number pe click karke underlying list, CSV export (watermark + logged).

Kahaan: Sidebar → Dashboard / Reports

Audit Log New

Har change, login, export, config — record. Koi delete nahi kar sakta (tamper-proof). Regulator/auditor ke liye.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Audit Log (CEO/Compliance/Auditor)

Recycle Bin Old+

Lead/task/document delete karo toh permanently nahi jaate — Recycle Bin se restore ho sakte hain.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Recycle Bin

Settings Old+

Company profile, stages, sources, doc types, Business Rules (eligibility caps + SLA + working-hours calendar), case-number format, web-to-lead API. Sab bina developer ke change ho.

Kahaan: Sidebar → Settings · Assignment Rules
